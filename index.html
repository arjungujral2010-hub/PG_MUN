About Conference

MORE THAN A CONFERENCE.
A PLATFORM FOR CHANGE.

PG-MUN 2026, organised by Dr. Pillai Global Academy, is a platform that brings together students from Grades 7 to 12 to experience the world of diplomacy, international relations, public speaking, debate and negotiation.

Over two exciting days, delegates will step into the roles of representatives and decision-makers, engage with global and contemporary issues, exchange perspectives, negotiate solutions and develop their leadership abilities.

PG-MUN aims to encourage young minds to think critically, communicate confidently and understand the importance of collaboration in an increasingly interconnected world.

Diplomacy & International Relations

Experience real multilateral dynamics, global treaties, and sovereign policy strategy.

Public Speaking & Debate

Articulate complex points of view under parliamentary procedure with clarity and poise.

Leadership & Negotiation

Build working coalitions, forge consensus, and co-author groundbreaking draft resolutions.

Research & Critical Thinking

Analyse deep geopolitical precedents, factual data points, and complex scenarios.

Itinerary

TWO DAYS OF DIPLOMACY AND DISCOVERY.

Follow the progression of committee sessions, diplomatic caucuses, awards, and the grand DJ celebration.

Day 1 • 21 November 2026

Delegate Registration & Kit Distribution

Delegates arrive at Dr. Pillai Global Academy, register their presence, collect conference kits, placards, and ID badges.

Day 1 • 21 November 2026

Grand Opening Ceremony

Welcome address by DPGA leadership, introduction of the Secretariat, official gavel banging, and declaration of PG-MUN 2026 open.

Day 1 • 21 November 2026

Committee Session 01 & GSL Establishment

Roll call, adoption of the agenda, establishment of General Speakers List (GSL), and opening policy speeches by delegates.

Day 1 • 21 November 2026

Formal Debate & Moderated Caucuses

Intense deliberations on specific agenda sub-topics, structured cross-questioning, and unmoderated bloc formations.

Day 1 • 21 November 2026

Further Committee Proceedings & Working Papers

Drafting of initial working papers, bilateral bloc consultations, and conclusion of Day 1 committee business.

Day 2 • 22 November 2026

Resumption of Committee Sessions

Roll call, crisis updates, presentation of working papers, and critical clause-by-clause review.

Day 2 • 22 November 2026

Debate, Amendments & Resolution Voting

Friendly and unfriendly amendments, voting procedure on draft resolutions, and concluding closing statements.

Day 2 • 22 November 2026

Closing Proceedings & Valedictory Ceremony

Executive Board feedback, addresses by distinguished dignitaries, and formal adjournment of committees.

Day 2 • 22 November 2026

Awards & Recognition Ceremony

Announcement of Best Delegate, High Commendation, and Honourable Mention trophies and certificates.

Day 2 • 22 November 2026

Grand DJ Celebration

The legendary delegate social and dance party to conclude two unforgettable days of diplomacy!

* Detailed session timings and the final conference schedule will be announced soon by the Secretariat.
