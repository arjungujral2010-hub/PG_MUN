Disarmament & International Security Committee

Committee Overview & Introduction

The Disarmament and International Security Committee (DISEC) is the First Committee of the United Nations General Assembly. Established under Chapter IV of the United Nations Charter, DISEC is mandated to deal with disarmament, global challenges, and threats to peace that affect the international community, seeking solutions to the challenges in the international security regime.

At PG-MUN 2026, DISEC operates under the principle of universal representation, where every sovereign state holds an equal vote. Delegates will navigate intricate geopolitical fault lines, non-proliferation architectures, autonomous weapons systems, cyber-warfare boundaries, and conventional arms regulations. The goal is to author comprehensive resolutions that enhance collective peace while respecting state sovereignty.

Delegate Preparation Guidelines

Arms Treaties & Conventions: Familiarize yourself with the NPT, ATT, Biological Weapons Convention, and outer space non-weaponization accords.

Geopolitical Blocs: Understand regional dynamics, military pacts, non-aligned perspectives, and defense expenditure priorities.

Speeches & Moderated Caucuses: Develop structured, persuasive arguments with factual evidence and strategic policy solutions.

Operative Clauses: Draft clear, enforceable measures focusing on confidence-building, international oversight, and technical assistance.
