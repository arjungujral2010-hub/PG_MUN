Conference Itinerary

TWO DAYS OF DIPLOMACY AND DISCOVERY.

Explore the conference timeline across 21st and 22nd November 2026. From opening roll calls to working papers, awards, and the post-summit celebration.

DAY 1 – 21 NOVEMBER 2026

Delegate Registration and Arrival

Delegates check in at the main registration concourse, collect official ID credentials, delegate placards, conference kits, and enjoy welcome refreshments before proceedings begin.

DAY 1 – 21 NOVEMBER 2026

Grand Opening Ceremony

Formal opening of PG-MUN 2026 in the Plenary Hall. Welcome address by the Secretariat and Leadership of Dr. Pillai Global Academy, introduction of Executive Boards, and ceremonial gavel strike.

DAY 1 – 21 NOVEMBER 2026

Committee Sessions Begin

Committees convene in dedicated council chambers. Formal roll call, setting of the substantive agenda, establishment of the General Speaker's List, and opening policy speeches.

DAY 1 – 21 NOVEMBER 2026

Formal Debate and Negotiations

High-intensity moderated caucuses examining specific sub-issues, accompanied by strategic unmoderated caucuses for bloc formation, informal consultation, and early working paper frameworks.

DAY 1 – 21 NOVEMBER 2026

Further Committee Proceedings & Evening Debrief

Continuation of debate, crisis updates, review of working paper initial drafts with the Executive Board, and committee adjournment for Day 1.

DAY 2 – 22 NOVEMBER 2026

Committee Sessions Reconvene

Second day opens with morning roll call, urgent crisis directives, and refinement of working papers into formal Draft Resolutions.

DAY 2 – 22 NOVEMBER 2026

Debate and Negotiations

Introduction of formal Draft Resolutions on the committee floor, rigorous questioning of sponsors, cross-examination, and submission of friendly and unfriendly amendments.

DAY 2 – 22 NOVEMBER 2026

Closing Proceedings & Voting Procedures

Committee enters voting procedure on all tabled Draft Resolutions and amendments. Final roll call votes, speeches for and against, and formal adoption of resolutions.

DAY 2 – 22 NOVEMBER 2026

Awards and Recognition Ceremony

Plenary Assembly gathers for closing remarks, Executive Board feedback, and presentation of prestigious trophies and certificates for Best Delegate, High Commendation, and Honourable Mentions.

DAY 2 – GRAND FINALE

Special DJ Celebration

The official post-conference celebration! Delegates unwind with concert lighting, music, networking, and celebration with new friends to conclude PG-MUN 2026.
