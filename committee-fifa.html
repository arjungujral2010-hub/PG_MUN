FÉDÉRATION INTERNATIONALE DE FOOTBALL ASSOCIATION (FIFA)

"Step into the world of international football governance, sporting diplomacy and decision-making. Explore the challenges and global influence of the beautiful game."

Sports Diplomacy & Integrity Forum

FIFA Supreme Council

Committee Overview & Introduction

Football is the world's most pervasive cultural phenomenon, uniting billions across borders, languages, and political divides. Behind the spectacle of the FIFA World Cup and continental tournaments lies an intricate governance framework that directly intersects with sovereign diplomacy, international economics, human rights, and sports integrity.

At PG-MUN 2026, the FIFA Council simulates the executive decision-making body of world football. Delegates represent continental confederations, member associations, regulatory bodies, and host-nation delegates. The committee will examine tournament bidding integrity, Financial Fair Play (FFP), athlete welfare, modern technologies in officiating (VAR/semi-automated offside), and the powerful role of sports in international cultural diplomacy.

Delegate Preparation Guidelines

FIFA Statutes & Legal Framework: Review the governing statutes, transfer matching system (TMS), and anti-doping regulations.

Economic Diplomacy: Evaluate the financial impact of tournament hosting, broadcast licensing, and grassroots football investments.

Stakeholder Negotiation: Balance national federation priorities with club interests, player rights, and fan community concerns.

Policy Innovation: Draft reformative resolutions that safeguard the long-term integrity, accessibility, and transparency of global football.
