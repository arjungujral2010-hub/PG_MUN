Dr. Pillai Global Academy is a world-class international school nestled in the educational hub of New Panvel. Built to provide holistic global education, the campus features acoustically treated committee chambers, a grand plenary auditorium, secure grounds, and Wi-Fi-enabled delegate research suites.

Campus Facilities & Amenities

Air-Conditioned Chambers: Individual conference rooms equipped for seamless committee debate and unmoderated caucuses.

Plenary Auditorium: Grand assembly hall for Opening and Closing Ceremonies, keynote addresses, and awards presentation.

Dining Commons: Dedicated dining areas serving nutritious meals, refreshments, and snacks across both days.

Safety & Security: 24/7 security surveillance, faculty chaperones, first aid assistance, and access-controlled entry gates.

Transit Guide

HOW TO REACH DR. PILLAI GLOBAL ACADEMY

Conveniently accessible via road, suburban rail, and airport links across the Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

By Suburban Railway

Panvel Railway Station: Major railway junction connected via Central and Harbour suburban lines (approx. 7–10 mins by auto-rickshaw).
Khandeshwar Station: Convenient suburban stop on the Harbour line (approx. 5 mins by auto-rickshaw).

By Road & Expressway

Direct connectivity via the Sion-Panvel Expressway from Mumbai, Chembur, and Vashi. Direct exit from the Mumbai-Pune Expressway for delegates travelling from Pune and Lonavala.

Airport Connectivity

Convenient access from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (CSMIA) in Mumbai (approx. 45–55 mins via Eastern Freeway/Sion-Panvel Highway) and the upcoming Navi Mumbai International Airport (NMIA).
