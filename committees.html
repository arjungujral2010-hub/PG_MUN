Delegate Guidance

HOW TO CHOOSE YOUR COMMITTEE

Find the council that best aligns with your interests, debate style, and diplomatic ambitions.

For Geopolitics & Crisis Management

UNSC & DISEC are ideal for delegates who thrive in high-stakes negotiations, multilateral security treaties, crisis updates, and sovereign statecraft.

For Ecology, Science & Global Policy

UNEP is tailored for delegates interested in planetary boundaries, climate justice, biodiversity conservation, sustainable tech, and international environmental law.

For Philosophy, Strategy & Historical Debate

Indian Mythology offers a rare, immersive arena to navigate moral dilemmas, code of war, alliances, and duty (Dharma) from legendary Indian epics.

For Sports Diplomacy & Global Governance

FIFA explores the intersection of international sports administration, multi-billion-dollar bids, athlete representation, and the geopolitics of football.
