Institutional Vision & Heritage

MORE THAN A CONFERENCE. A PLATFORM FOR CHANGE.

"PG-MUN 2026, organised by Dr. Pillai Global Academy, is a platform that brings together students from Grades 7 to 12 to experience the world of diplomacy, international relations, public speaking, debate and negotiation."

Our Mission

NURTURING TOMORROW'S GLOBAL DIPLOMATS

Over two exciting days, delegates will step into the roles of representatives and decision-makers, engage with global and contemporary issues, exchange perspectives, negotiate solutions and develop their leadership abilities.

PG-MUN aims to encourage young minds to think critically, communicate confidently and understand the importance of collaboration in an increasingly interconnected world. Hosted within the prestigious campus of Dr. Pillai Global Academy (DPGA) in New Panvel, this conference is engineered to simulate authentic multilateral summits with university-grade procedural rigor, high-calibre academic materials, and an atmosphere charged with intellectual debate.

Diplomacy & International Relations

Understand sovereign foreign policy, multilateral treaties, consensus building, and the complexities of international diplomacy.

Public Speaking & Debate

Overcome stage anxiety, hone persuasive oratory, master structured rebuttals, and address substantive assemblies with confidence.

Leadership & Negotiation

Learn how to build diplomatic coalitions in unmoderated caucuses, reconcile opposing national interests, and draft resolution clauses.

Research & Critical Thinking

Analyze primary sources, United Nations resolutions, economic statistics, and geopolitical doctrines to author evidence-based solutions.
