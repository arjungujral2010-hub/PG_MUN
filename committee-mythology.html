INDIAN MYTHOLOGY: KURUKSHETRA COUNCIL

"Enter a world of legendary narratives, powerful characters and complex moral dilemmas. Explore iconic stories, perspectives, conflicts and decisions through an immersive committee experience."

Kurukshetra • Rules of War • Dharma

Indian Mythology: The Great Sabha

Committee Overview & Introduction

The Indian epics represent some of the most profound inquiries into human nature, ethical philosophy, statecraft, and the tragic consequences of geopolitical conflict ever recorded. At the crossroads of Kurukshetra, questions of duty (Dharma), righteousness, allegiance, kinship, and the rules of warfare collide with unyielding force.

In this specialized simulation at PG-MUN 2026, delegates step into the roles of legendary warriors, kings, philosophers, and ministers. Rather than a modern parliamentary debate, this committee functions as an immersive historical council where delegates must defend ancestral vows, weigh ethical dilemmas of statecraft, debate the laws of just war (Dharma Yuddha), and navigate shifting alliances that determine the fate of kingdoms.

Character Immersion: Thoroughly study your persona's motivations, philosophical dilemmas, familial ties, and historical choices.

Dharmic Statecraft: Balance moral philosophy with tactical survival, questioning established conventions while crafting novel solutions.

Eloquent Rhetoric: Hone dramatic oratory, allegorical references, and strategic persuasion to sway the Great Council.

Crisis Directives: Respond to surprise battlefield updates, shifting kingdom allegiances, and divine proclamations in real time.
