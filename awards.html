Evaluation Framework

HOW COMMITTEES ARE EVALUATED

Executive Boards assess delegates across a standardized, transparent rubric measuring true diplomatic acumen.

1. Foreign Policy & Portfolio Fidelity

Accurate, consistent adherence to your allocated country or character's strategic worldview, past voting record, and constitutional mandate.

2. Oratory & Substantive Speeches

Clarity, rhetorical persuasiveness, factual backing, and tactical timing during General Speaker's List speeches and Moderated Caucuses.

3. Negotiation & Coalition Building

Leadership exhibited in Unmoderated Caucuses, ability to unite differing geopolitical blocs, and respectful, collaborative statesmanship.

4. Resolution Authorship & Drafting

Direct contribution to working papers, innovative perambulatory and operative clauses, and procedural command during amendment debates.
